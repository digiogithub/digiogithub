## About me

Software engineer. I joined DIGIO after university (Universidad de Murcia) and have led technology and R&D there since **2007** — plus several subsidiaries and products along the way.

I work from the web-application and infrastructure side: product architecture, team leadership, and shipping systems that have to hold up under real traffic. In recent years I have been focused on **Go**, **Rust**, and **Crystal**, plus **artificial intelligence** and **IoT**.

Previously I have been CTO / technical lead on products such as Symposium (Emagister), PocketAcademy, and projects for the City of Murcia, the regional government of Murcia, and Grupo Intercom.
